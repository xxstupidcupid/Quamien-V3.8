Market methodology

Global reference markets: S&P 500, Nasdaq Composite, FTSE 100, Nikkei 225, Hang Seng, and EURO STOXX 50. Sector sensitivities vary by company. Local conditions and event-driven news can amplify or offset global movements. Market prices are fictional simulation values; global benchmarks are real-world reference data.

Runs locally in your browser and requires no account. An internet connection is needed to refresh live global stock benchmarks; the rest of the simulator and saved market data remain available offline. This is a fictional-world tool, not a real government service.
